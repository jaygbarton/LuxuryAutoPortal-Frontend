# Golden Luxury Auto project guidance

Read [docs/project-vision.md](docs/project-vision.md) when planning product or architecture changes. It records the owner's long-term direction and the boundary between approved work and future ideas.

Golden Luxury Auto is funding a software platform for rental companies, using its own operations to develop and improve it. Treat the product as a centralized rental-business operating system, with practical AI integration, including GoHighLevel where useful.

The owner eventually wants to sell or license access to other rental companies. Do not build the commercial or multi-company version until explicitly requested. The AI opportunities in the vision document are suggestions, not implementation authorization.

Preserve other developers' changes. Inspect the current branch and remote state before integrating or publishing work; do not overwrite unrelated changes.
